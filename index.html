Claude Gmail

Claude Gmail is a private, personal tool. Its owner uses it on their own computer to organize their own Gmail accounts: finding old promotional mail, newsletters and notifications, moving it to Trash, and creating mail filters.

It is not offered to the public, and only the owner's own Google accounts use it.

Claude Gmail 是個人自用的工具，只用來整理擁有者自己的 Gmail 信箱（找出舊的廣告、電子報與通知，移到垃圾桶，並建立篩選器），不對外提供服務。
